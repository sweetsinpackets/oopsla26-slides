Limitations of Reachability Types

No bulk deallocation. cells can be grouped, but not deallocated together
No lifetime control. reclamation is left to garbage collection
No cyclic store structures: cells cannot reach later ones (telescoping). a cell may reach itself, but no cycle spans several cells

  val c2 = new Ref(f)         //: Ref[(Int -> Int)??~{q2}??]??~{c2}??
